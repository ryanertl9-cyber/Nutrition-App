Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Nutrient Ledger is a food and nutrient tracker. This page explains what it stores, where, and what you can do about it. In short: we keep only what the app needs to work, we never sell or share it for advertising, and you can delete everything at any time.

Without an account

If you use the app without signing in, everything you enter stays in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account, we store

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; nobody can read it.
  • Your profile: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, unit preference and any custom nutrient targets. These are used only to calculate your targets.
  • Your food log, custom foods and recipes, so they sync across your devices.

Food logs and body measurements can reveal information about your health. We treat all of it as sensitive and use it only to show it back to you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't use advertising or third-party tracking cookies or analytics.
  • We don't look at your log, except if you ask us for help with a problem.

Where it's kept

  • Supabase stores accounts and synced data. Database rules ensure each account can read and change only its own records.
  • Netlify serves the website. Like any web host it processes your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and keep them secure.
  • USDA FoodData Central: if you use online food search, your search words (not your log or account) are sent to the U.S. Department of Agriculture.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your browser also keeps a copy so the app works offline; signing out removes it from that device.

Your choices

  • Export your data at any time: Profile → Your data → Export backup.
  • Correct anything in the app directly.
  • Delete your account and all its data: Account → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Washington or the EU/UK), you may have further rights to access, delete or restrict your data. You can exercise them with the tools above or by contacting us.

Children

Nutrient Ledger isn't intended for children under 13, and accounts may not be created for them.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and show a notice in the app.
